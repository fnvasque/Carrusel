# Sistema de memoria agentica en IA: tres niveles

## Qué es

El post describe un sistema de memoria agentica en tres niveles para agentes de IA, destacando la importancia de la orquestación de agentes.

## Ideas clave

- La memoria a corto plazo maneja el contexto inmediato y tareas activas.
- La memoria a medio plazo almacena conocimiento relevante para varias tareas.
- La memoria a largo plazo guarda información persistente e histórica.
- El flujo del sistema incluye capturar, clasificar, almacenar, recuperar y reflexionar.
- La orquestación de agentes es crucial para el funcionamiento eficiente.

> 3-Tier AI Agentic Memory.
> Agents need memory to be useful.
> But not all memories are equal.
> Organize memory in 3 tiers for speed, depth, and longevity.
> Active, Context
> Relevant Knowledge
> Enduring Knowledge
> 01 SHORT-TERM (Working Memory)
> What's happening right now.
> Fast
> Small
> Ephemeral
> 02 MID-TERM (Semantic Memory)
> What matters across tasks.
> Relevant
> Searchable
> Updatable
> 03 LONG-TERM (Persistent Memory)
> What lasts a long time.
> Durable
> Scalable
> Historical
> THE FLOW
> Perceive / Receive
> Store in Right Tier
> Retrieve When Needed
> Reflect & Update
> HOW IT WORKS
> 1. Ingest
> Capture inputs from environment & actions.
> 2. Classify
> Decide which tier it belongs to.
> 3. Store
> Save in the appropriate memory tier.
> 4. Retrieve
> Fetch the right memory when needed.
> 5. Reflect
> Summarize, link, and promote important features.
> EXAMPLE IN ACTION
> User asks: “Book a flight to Paris next Friday.”
> Stored in SHORT-TERM (current task context)
> Flight prefs, past bookings stored in MID-TERM
> Travel history, loyalty info stored in LONG-TERM
> Agent uses all 3 tiers to plan the best response.
> TIER COMPARISON
> SHORT-TERM (Working)
> Purpose
> Handle immediate context
> Scope
> Current session / task
> Size
> Small
> Speed
> Very Fast
> Lifetime
> Seconds – Minutes
> Example
> Chat history, latest tool outputs
> MID-TERM (Semantic)
> Purpose
> Capture important knowledge
> Scope
> Across tasks
> Size
> Medium
> Speed
> Fast
> Lifetime
> Hours – Days
> Example
> User preferences, facts, summaries
> LONG-TERM (Persistent)
> Purpose
> Store everything durable
> Scope
> Across lifetime
> Size
> Large
> Speed
> Medium
> Lifetime
> Months – Forever
> Example
> Past interactions, logs, documents
> Good memory makes agents feel smart.
> DESIGN PRINCIPLES
> Speed Where It Matters
> Keep short-term fast and lean.
> Relevance Over Everything
> Store what’s useful, not everything.
> Promote Wisely
> Move memories up when they matter.
> Protect Privacy
> Respect user data at every tier.
> Build for Lifespan
> Think long-term memory on every tier.
> Right memory, right time, right impact.
