## Qué es

El post describe cómo Roman utiliza un sistema de marketing llamado "content thrifting" para generar ingresos significativos con su plataforma de empleo.

## Ideas clave

- Roman genera $28K al mes con 779 suscriptores activos.
- Utiliza "content thrifting" para identificar patrones de contenido repetibles.
- Contrata creadores mayores de 50 años para aumentar la credibilidad.
- Implementa un muro de pago estricto sin plan gratuito.
- Su stack tecnológico incluye herramientas como Shortimize y PostHog.

## Herramientas

- **Shortimize** — Optimización de contenido corto.
- **PostHog** — Análisis de datos y comportamiento de usuarios.
- **WhatsApp** — Comunicación con creadores.
- **Clarity** — Análisis de experiencia de usuario.
- **Hetzner** — Alojamiento web.
- **Postmark** — Envío de correos electrónicos transaccionales.
- **Cloudflare** — Seguridad y rendimiento web.

> My Growth Playbook for CareerHound:
> 
> Phase 1: Idea Sourcing & Product Simplification
> Mine Proven Markets: Find products already making money on platforms like Sideshift or Sensor Tower. Keep It Simple: Turn a basic solution into a clean, one-page website with a clear value proposition.
> 
> Phase 2: Content Thrifting & Pattern Spotting
> Deep Research: Study top posts in your niche across TikTok, Instagram, and Reddit. Find Timeless Angles: Skip short-lived trends; focus on repeatable content patterns that stay relevant for years.
> 
> Phase 3: Scalable Creator Operations
> Target the Right Creators: DM creators who match your target demographic to build instant trust. Streamline Production: Give creators dead-simple templates so you can pay low rates ($20/video) for steady output.
> 
> Phase 4: Monetization & Daily Experimentation
> Hard Paywalls: Use paid-only plans ($7/week, $20/month) to drive immediate revenue. Daily Sprints: Test marketing angles daily, learn from failed experiments, and double down on winning formats.

> Yet Roman’s Career Hound pulled $28K last month with just 779 active subs and 11.7M social views in 30 days.

> Hard paywall. $7/week, $20/month, or $50/year. No free plan. 31% churn - and it still prints.
