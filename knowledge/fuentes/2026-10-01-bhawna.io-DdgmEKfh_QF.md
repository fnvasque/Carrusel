## Qué es

El post destaca las habilidades esenciales para desarrollar agentes de IA efectivos en 2027, enfocándose en la ingeniería de sistemas, bucles, contexto, herramientas, memoria, orquestación, y más.

## Ideas clave

- La ingeniería de sistemas es crucial para controlar agentes de IA.
- Diseñar bucles efectivos evita que los agentes se repitan innecesariamente.
- El contexto adecuado es vital para respuestas precisas de los agentes.
- Las herramientas deben ser claras y manejables para los agentes.
- La memoria debe ser estructurada en corto, largo plazo y semántica.

> Agentic AI Concepts You Must Master By 2027

> [!quote]- Caption original
> The real skill in 2027 is systems thinking.
> 
> Harness Engineering: Give your agent a safe, observable stack with retries, sandboxing, and cost limits so it doesn’t spiral out of control.
> 
> Loop Engineering: Design how your agent repeats actions, checks its own progress, and knows when to stop instead of looping forever.
> 
> Context Engineering: Feed the model the right info at the right time. Too much context rots, too little breaks the response.
> Tool Design: Build tools your agent can actually use correctly, with clear names, strict input schemas, and error handling it can recover from.
> 
> Memory Architecture: Help your agent remember what matters using short-term, long-term, and semantic memory instead of dumping everything into one context window.
> 
> Orchestration Patterns: Coordinate multiple agents so they split tasks, hand off work, and combine results without stepping on each other.
> 
> Guardrails & Permissions: Keep your agent useful but safe by scoping what tools it can touch and limiting blast radius if something goes wrong.
> 
> Evals for Agents: Test your agent before you trust it. Check outcomes, not just outputs, and catch regressions before they ship.
> 
> Human-in-the-Loop Design: Know exactly when a human should step in for approval, and when your agent should just run.
> 
> Observability & Tracing: See every step your agent takes so you can debug fast and ship with confidence instead of guessing.
> 
> Master these 10 and you’ll be building agents that actually hold up in production, not just demos that break on day two.
